Assumptions and sources
  • Canadian fixed-rate convention: the quoted rate compounds semi-annually, payments are monthly. The rate is held constant for the full amortization (no renewal risk modelled).
  • Welcome tax (droits de mutation), 2026 brackets. Montreal: 0.5% to $62,900; 1% to $315,000; 1.5% to $552,300; 2% to $1,104,700; 2.5% to $2,136,500; 3.5% to $3,113,000; 4% above. "Elsewhere in Quebec" uses the provincial default (0.5% / 1% / 1.5% above $315,000); some municipalities add higher brackets above $500k. Tax base assumed equal to purchase price.
  • Mortgage default insurance (CMHC) when down payment is under 20%: 4.00% (5–9.99% down), 3.10% (10–14.99%), 2.80% (15–19.99%), plus 0.20% for amortizations over 25 years. Premium is added to the loan; Quebec's 9% tax on the premium is paid in cash at closing.
  • Minimum down payment: 5% of the first $500,000, 10% of the portion up to $1.5M, 20% at $1.5M and above.
  • Property + school tax is approximated as a flat % of purchase price; check the actual assessment and borough rate for a real listing.
  • Not included: appreciation, maintenance, home insurance, condo fees, utilities, opportunity cost of the down payment, first-time buyer tax credits.
